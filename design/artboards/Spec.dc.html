NeonMeter · design decisions, approved 2026-10-03, as shipped in 1.0.0

What the band shows, and how it moves

In the terminal the band is one Client of monospace cells; on the desktop the hooks module draws the text and smooth vector bars under a glowing halo. One builder serves both.

Layout: edge to edge

RuleDecision
WidthThe row is exactly bodyColumns wide: first cell to last cell, no leading space, nothing empty on the right.
With barsText is fixed; the bars share the free cells evenly (the leftover cells go to the first bars). Percent, reset and token fields are padded to fixed widths so the bars do not jump as numbers change. The last segment is not padded.
Without barsSegments spread apart: the free cells become equal gaps with a centered ·.
Tiers by fit, not fixed breakpointsfull while every bar gets 8 or more cells; else compact while every bar gets 4 or more; else narrow (numbers spread, gaps of at least 3); else micro (truncate-end). With three bars that switches at 96 / 65 / 36 columns (full from 96, compact from 65, narrow from 36); with context alone, full holds down to 31.
Order5-hour, Weekly, Spend (only when the gateway reports one; never a bar), Context.

Bars and the pulse

ItemDecision
Cell glyph━ for filled and empty terminal cells, so a bar reads as one continuous line (the glyph option takes any single-width character). Filled = round(percent / 100 x length), at least 1 when above 0, capped at length.
Coloringramp (default): the filled part runs through every range from the first up to the percent's, in equal stretches, so the bar always ends in the percent's color and shows every range below it: 85% runs blue, lime, green, yellow, orangered. level (barColoring: "level"): every filled cell takes the percent's range color.
DesktopFull labels and fields as text; each bar a smooth bar with rounded ends (desktopBars: "bars", the default) or a row of dots, drawn as vectors. Every filled bar, dot and percent sits on a halo of its own color that widens with the pulse (glow).
Pulse (preview)A status-light pulse: 0.8 s, ease-in-out, infinite, glow 6px/2px at rest to 12px/5px at the peak, in the cell's own color. Applies to filled cells and to every percent.
Pulse (what cells can do)A terminal has no glow. The band is one Client whose own timer on the surface frame clock steps 8 frames per cycle: each live cell and percent blends toward white, up to 45% at the peak, and back. No host round trip per frame. On the desktop the pulse is an animation inside each drawing.
StaleA reading is stale when a fetch fails or it is older than 2 x pollSeconds. Window bars and percents freeze (no pulse) at 45% strength; stale 5m italic after the last window on full and compact, showing the reading's age in whole minutes (1m, 2m ... 59m, 1h, 1h05m, then 1d2h), ~ before each window percent on narrow and micro. Context keeps pulsing. The pulse never pauses for any other reason.
First loadWindow bars are all grey loading cells, pulsing; the percent shows ….
No subscriptionContext alone, stretched across the whole row.

isWorking / hasSurveyNo change / yield with next(e).

IdleThe pulse runs whenever a reading is live; it stops only for stale data.

Theme and settings

ItemDecision
Following the themeThe mod reads Claude Code's own theme row with $.config.list() at session start and re-reads it when a config.set on theme passes, so a theme switch re-picks the ramp live. A value naming light picks the light ramp, one naming dark the dark ramp.
When the theme cannot be readtheme: "auto" (the default) falls back to dark; dark or light forces one. The desktop follows the app's own appearance under desktopTheme: "auto".
ToastsNone. The band is the only surface.
