NeonMeter · palette

Six neon ranges, two themes, one pulse

Neon on dark. On white a true neon washes out, so the light theme keeps each hue at full saturation but darkens it to at least 3:1 (yellow goes to dark gold to get there). Terminal cells are heavy lines (━) that run into one continuous bar, empty ones in GitHub's empty-day grey; the desktop draws the same colors as smooth bars under a glowing halo. Every live cell and every percent pulses like a status light: 0.8 s, ease-in-out, glow 6px/2px to 12px/5px in its own color.
